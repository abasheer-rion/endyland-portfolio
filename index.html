About

I am studying electrical engineering at UT Dallas. In the RIDE program, a team of three designed and simulated a 5.8 GHz antenna array that finds where a signal comes from and steers its beam toward it. The receive board is designed and not yet built. Read about the direction finder.

I spent two summers at Verizon in Richardson, Texas, on software and automation, and I worked as an automation engineer at RION Solutions. Read about Verizon.

I was lead engineer at Argus, which makes software for convenience stores and is built heavily on computer vision. While I was there it presented at a Demo Day, joined NVIDIA Inception and the Cloudflare Startup Program, and was verified into POS systems. I handed off my work and left in 2026 to spend more time on school and electrical engineering. Argus continues under Rayan Sikkandar and the team. Read about Argus.

HackUTD 2026 was my first hackathon. My team won two awards, including 3rd place in the State Farm track, with Safe Home. It brings home sensor readings and nearby disaster alerts into one place so response teams can prioritise survivors without power or Wi-Fi. The team was Adyaan Akram, Saif Yusuff, Dhruv Patel and me. Read about Safe Home.
