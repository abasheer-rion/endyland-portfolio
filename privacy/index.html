Who runs this site

endyland.com is the personal portfolio of Ayan Basheer, based in Dallas, Texas. I run it myself. There is no company behind it. You can reach me at ayanbasheer@gmail.com.

What the site collects

Nothing on purpose. The site has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no comments and no ads. It does not set cookies. It does not build profiles or sell or share data.

The only data that reaches me is what any web host sees when a page loads, covered in the next two sections.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any host, Cloudflare handles your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time of the request, so it can deliver the site and keep it secure. Those server logs belong to Cloudflare. I do not run my own log database and I do not use them to identify visitors.

Cloudflare may also count page views with its privacy-focused Web Analytics. It gives me totals such as visits and countries. It does not use cookies or track you across other sites. Cloudflare explains how it handles this in its own privacy policy.

The site links to GitHub, LinkedIn and sites I have built. Once you follow a link, that site’s own policy applies, not this one.

What stays in your browser

When you move between pages, the site saves the spot where you clicked in your browser’s session storage, under the name endyland-vt-point. It is used only to start the page transition animation from that spot. It stays on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and disappears when you close the tab.

No cookies, no local storage and no tracking identifiers are used.

Email

The site shows a mailto link. If you email ayanbasheer@gmail.com, I get your address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply, and I do not add you to any list.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Asking me to delete something

I hold no personal data about visitors, so there is normally nothing to delete. If you emailed me and want that thread removed, write to ayanbasheer@gmail.com and I will delete it. For Cloudflare logs, contact Cloudflare directly.

Changes

If the site starts doing something new with data, I will update this page and the date at the top before it goes live.

This is a plain-language privacy policy, not legal advice. It describes how this personal site works today.
