Research

Four projects: an antenna array design for finding and steering toward a 5.8 GHz signal, a test bench for trading strategies, a rent model, and a search tool for county spending.

RF direction finder and Butler matrix

A design for a 5.8 GHz antenna array that estimates the direction a signal arrives from and steers its beam to it. The first array has four antennas in a 2x2 grid and the larger one has sixteen in a 4x4 grid. It is a UT Dallas RIDE project with two other students, September 2026 to now. All results so far are simulated. In simulation the 4x4 version finds a source to 0.022 degrees at 20 dB signal to noise, 4.5 times closer than the 2x2.

Parity

A test bench that runs trading strategies through 11 fixed tests and compares them with holding the S&P 500. 16 strategies have been tested. None passed. The best Sharpe ratio is 0.75, against 0.54 for the market. I built the test engine and the web app on a small team, October 2026 to now.

DFW rental pricing model

A model that predicts Dallas-Fort Worth rents from 1,200+ listings. The median error is 6%. It flagged 8 units asking far less than predicted, worth about $14K a year.

Public spending data explorer

A search tool for county spending records. It cleans 250K+ rows and merges 40+ vendors that appear under several names. A search that took about 20 minutes by hand takes under 5 seconds.
