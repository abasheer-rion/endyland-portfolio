RF direction finder and Butler matrix

UT Dallas RIDE. Sep 2026 to now. One of 3 on the team.

A 5.8 GHz antenna array that finds where a signal comes from, then steers its beam toward it. Includes a Butler matrix that steers the beam with fixed phase shifts, so no phase has to be measured.

4x4 array error
0.022 degrees at 20 dB
Versus the 2x2
4.5 times smaller
Steering loss
under 1.1 dB on average with 2-bit phase shifters
