Argus Dashboard

The Argus store portal. Sales, transactions, staff on shift, stock, alerts and cameras on one screen, on top of one API.

Developer, about 70% of the commits, from December 2025 until I left in 2026. JavaScript, Node and Express, Postgres, Clerk, Stripe and Cloudflare Pages.

  • Store portal

From NEXUS to Argus

RION already had a client portal called NEXUS, built as several separate apps. I turned it into one store screen and renamed it Argus. Underneath it keeps six pieces: a store portal, an admin app, a phone app, an ops app, a status page and bulk email.

The demo screen shows sales today and by hour, transactions, who is on shift, what is running low and alerts with a dollar amount next to each. Later work added camera monitoring, links to the register (POS) and to QuickBooks, invoice emails, faster pages and alerts. The payments side of NEXUS is on the NEXUS Command Center page.

Architecture

Every front end talks to one Node and Express API. The API keeps its data in Postgres on Hetzner, signs people in with Clerk and bills through Stripe. The Argus engine sends alerts to the API as HMAC-signed webhooks. The API stores the incident and pushes a Socket.io notification to the portal.
