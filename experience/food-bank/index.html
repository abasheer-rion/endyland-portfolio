Food bank coordinator

I lead the volunteers who pack food for 400+ families a month.

Role
Food bank coordinator
Dates
Jun 2022 to now
Place
IAA and IACC
Work
  • Volunteer scheduling
  • Packing line

The problem

The IAA and IACC food bank serves 400+ families a month. Packing that much food takes volunteers who know their job and a line that keeps moving.

I started as a volunteer in June 2022 and became the coordinator. The job is to keep the people and the process in good shape every month.

What I did

I schedule and lead 5+ volunteers. I looked at how the packing line worked and redesigned the workflow, which made packing 15% faster.

The same volunteers now move more food in the same time, so more of each shift goes to families and less to waiting.

  • 15%faster packing

Three stages, from a volunteer shift to a family’s table.

Results

Packing is 15% faster, with the same volunteers, for 400+ families a month.

What I owned

  • Lead 5+ volunteers serving 400+ families a month.

What I learned

  • A change to the process can be worth as much as extra hands. A faster line helps every shift, not just one.
  • Volunteers stay when the job is clear. Scheduling and a simple, repeatable line matter as much as enthusiasm.
