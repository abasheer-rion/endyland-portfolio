Swadesi Mangoes

A storefront for a seasonal mango importer, rebuilt in Next.js with Shopify behind it. The rebuild is not live.

Main developer, April 2026. Next.js, Tailwind, Framer Motion and the Shopify Storefront API.

  • swadesimangoes.com

What it is

Swadesi Mangoes imports Indian mangoes and ships them across the US. It sells for a few months each year, so sold-out items, a perishable-goods policy and a wholesale channel all have to be clear to a visitor quickly.

The live store runs a Shopify theme with eight pages: home, buy mangoes, history, journey, disclaimer, know us, wholesale and contact. The screenshots on this page show that store. My version replaces the pages visitors see and leaves the catalog, cart and checkout in Shopify.

How it fits together

The cart is created through Shopify, and the Checkout button hands the shopper to Shopify’s own checkout. Payment details never reach the custom site, and the owner keeps managing orders where they already do.

What I did

  • Rebuilt the storefront in Next.js and connected products and the cart to the Shopify Storefront API.
  • Built a cart drawer, a sticky add-to-cart bar and product pages.
  • Added journey, about, wholesale and contact pages, and the policy pages.
  • Added a season switch and a banner that says when orders are open.
