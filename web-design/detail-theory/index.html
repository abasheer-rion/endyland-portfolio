The problem

A detailer who drives to the customer needs a website that books the job, not one that only looks good.

Detail Theory is a premium mobile detailing company in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Customers get hand detailing, ceramic coatings and paint correction in their own driveway. The owner needed a site that shows the finish, answers the first questions fast, and turns a visit into a confirmed slot without a phone tag.

Approach

I kept it small on purpose: one hand-written page, no framework, and two small server handlers.

  • One page. Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is nothing to build, so a change is a file edit.
  • A step-by-step booking form, because a long form on a phone loses people.
  • Live proof. A reviews strip that reads the business's Google rating, so the 5.0 on the page is not typed in by hand.
  • Cloudflare Pages for hosting, with the two handlers running as Pages Functions.

How it works

The relay sits between the page and the Google script so the secret never ships to the browser. The same idea covers reviews: a second handler asks Google Places for the business rating and review text, and the page shows a live strip. The Google key is stored as a Cloudflare Pages secret.

The site explains the service as three steps: book online or call, we come to you, drive away spotless. It says no water or power is needed from the customer, and a detail takes 90 minutes to 3 hours.

What I owned

  • Built the one-page site by hand, with no framework.
  • Built the step-by-step booking form. Bookings land straight in the business's Google Calendar.
  • Added a live Google reviews strip with the 5.0 rating.
  • Moved hosting to Cloudflare Pages.

What I learned

  • A small site without a framework is easy to hand to a client and easy to move between hosts.
  • Keep every secret on the server side of the page. The form and the reviews strip both go through a handler for that reason.
  • Showing a live rating builds more trust than a quote, and it keeps itself current.
